Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Renting on Rentivo is simple: browse our catalog, select a product, choose your rental dates, and complete your booking. We'll deliver the product to your door (or you can pick it up). When your rental period is over, return the product and your security deposit will be refunded.
The security deposit is a refundable amount held during your rental period. It protects against damage or loss. The deposit is returned in full once the product is returned in good condition. Deposit amounts vary by product and are clearly shown before booking.
You can return your rental by scheduling a pickup through your My Rentals page, or by dropping it off at any Rentivo hub location. Returns must be made by the end of your rental period. Late returns may incur additional daily charges.
Yes! You can extend your rental directly from the Rental Details page. Click "Extend Rental" and select your new end date. Extension pricing is calculated at the same daily/weekly rate. Extensions are subject to product availability.
We offer three delivery options: Standard Delivery (2-3 business days, $15), Express Delivery (next business day, $30), and Store Pickup (free, at your nearest Rentivo hub). All delivery options include tracking so you know exactly when your rental will arrive.
Cancellations made 48+ hours before the rental start date receive a full refund. Cancellations within 24-48 hours receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 24 hours of the start date are non-refundable. Security deposits are always refunded in full upon cancellation.
Normal wear and tear is expected and covered. For significant damage, repair costs will be deducted from your security deposit. If repair costs exceed the deposit, you'll be charged the difference. We recommend handling all products with reasonable care and reporting any issues immediately.
Once the product is returned and inspected (typically within 24 hours), your deposit refund will be initiated. Credit card refunds usually appear within 5-7 business days. UPI and wallet refunds are typically processed within 2-3 business days.
